HuntBot Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

What we collect

HuntBot reads job listing content (title, company, description) from Naukri.com and Instahyre.com pages you visit.

How we use it

Captured job data is saved only to your own personal Supabase database. We do not share your data with anyone.

What we store locally

Your API key and Supabase user ID are stored locally in your browser using Chrome storage.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party.
